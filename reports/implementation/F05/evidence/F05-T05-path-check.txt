F05-T05 — Evidencia 1/9: verificación de la ruta real de temporary/ (G-11)
Fecha: 2026-11-18 · Rol: Tester · Loop 1/3
Rutas anonimizadas: <PROJECT_ROOT> = raíz autorizada del proyecto.

COMANDO 1 (PowerShell):
  $root = (Resolve-Path .).Path
  $tmp = Join-Path $root 'temporary'
  Write-Output "ROOT=$root"; Write-Output "TEMP_PATH=$tmp"
  Write-Output "EXISTS=$(Test-Path -LiteralPath $tmp)"
  $items = @(Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath $tmp -Force)
  Write-Output "ITEM_COUNT=$($items.Count)"

SALIDA 1:
  ROOT=<PROJECT_ROOT>
  TEMP_PATH=<PROJECT_ROOT>\temporary
  EXISTS=True
  ITEM_COUNT=67
EXIT_CODE=0

COMANDO 2 (PowerShell — conteo recursivo y ubicación):
  $rec = @(Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath $tmp -Force -Recurse)
  $files = @($rec | Where-Object { -not $_.PSIsContainer })
  Write-Output "TOP=... RECURSIVE=$($rec.Count)"
  Write-Output "FILES=$($files.Count) DIRS=$($rec.Count - $files.Count)"
  $tmp2 = (Get-Item -LiteralPath $tmp)
  Write-Output "FULLNAME=$($tmp2.FullName)"; Write-Output "PARENT=$($tmp2.Parent.FullName)"
  Write-Output "IS_UNDER_ROOT=$($tmp2.FullName.StartsWith($root))"

SALIDA 2:
  TOP=67 RECURSIVE=73
  FILES=71 DIRS=2
  FULLNAME=<PROJECT_ROOT>\temporary
  PARENT=<PROJECT_ROOT>
  IS_UNDER_ROOT=True
EXIT_CODE=0
  -> 73 ítems en total (67 en el nivel superior + 6 dentro de 2 subdirectorios).
